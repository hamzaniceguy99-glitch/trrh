Program 01 · Allergens on request

't smulhoekske, Tielt

Allergen information is a legal duty for food that is not pre-packed, not just for labelled products, and it is the duty most often fudged in small kitchens with a vague reassurance. We give it in writing on request. What we will not do is promise the absence of traces in a kitchen that handles those ingredients, because that is a promise nobody in a small kitchen can keep.

Program 02 · Registered and certified

The Cozy Relax Home, Beernem

Flemish law requires tourist accommodation to be notified and to hold a fire safety certificate from the municipality, and plenty of lets operate without one because guests never ask. Ours is on file and we will produce it. We also collect and remit the tourist tax where the commune levies it, rather than treating it as an optional extra.

Program 03 · Licence category checked

Vehicle and caravan rental

This is the one that catches people out. A category B licence does not cover every combination of car and trailer: above certain weights you need a BE licence, and a renter driving without it is uninsured and committing an offence. We check the category against the combination and we will refuse the hire rather than hand over keys that put you in that position.

  • Per rental
  • Licensed drivers
  • Licence verified
  • Written agreement
