How we work

A small company running several things properly

TRH is a company incorporated under Belgian law, registered with the Crossroads Bank for Enterprises under enterprise number 1003.651.674, based at Lammersdam in 8750 Wingene and trading since December 2023 with a handful of staff. It runs 't smulhoekske, a limited-service eatery with a small shop in Tielt, The Cozy Relax Home, a holiday house in Beernem, and the rental of light vehicles and caravans from Wingene.

Running four things means four different sets of rules, and the honest approach is to name the one that applies rather than hope nobody asks. Tourist accommodation in Flanders must be registered and must hold a fire safety certificate from the municipality, and we will produce ours to a guest on request. Food that is not pre-packed still carries a legal duty to declare the fourteen listed allergens, which we do in writing, while being straight that traces cannot be guaranteed in a small kitchen. And a category B licence does not cover every car and trailer combination: above certain weights a BE licence is required, so we check the category against the combination and refuse the hire if it does not match.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Why should I ask a holiday let for a fire safety certificate?

Because Flemish law requires tourist accommodation to be registered and to hold one issued by the municipality, and operating without it is an offence. Guests almost never ask, which is exactly why some lets do not have one. Ours is on file and we will show it to you. It is a reasonable question to put to any accommodation you book.

I have a nut allergy. Can you tell me what is safe?

We can give you the allergen information in writing for everything we serve, including food that is not pre-packed, and that is a legal duty rather than a courtesy. What we cannot do is guarantee the absence of traces, because those ingredients are handled in the same kitchen. We would rather tell you that than offer a reassurance you might rely on.

I booked a caravan but you say I cannot tow it. Why?

Because a category B licence does not cover every combination. Once the maximum authorised mass of the car and trailer together, or of the trailer itself, passes the limits attached to category B, you need a BE licence. Driving without it means no insurance and an offence. We check before handing over keys, and we will find you a combination you can legally tow.

Can I cancel a stay within fourteen days like an online order?

Not automatically, and we would rather say so than let you assume it. The statutory fourteen-day cooling-off period for distance contracts does not apply to accommodation or to vehicle rental booked for specific dates. Our own cancellation terms apply, and we state them at the time of booking rather than in small print afterwards.

Do you add the tourist tax at the end?

We collect and remit it where the commune levies it, and we tell you about it when you book rather than presenting it as a surprise on arrival. It is a municipal charge rather than our margin, and treating it as an optional extra would simply mean we were not paying it.

Why do one company's websites cover food, stays and caravans?

Because it is genuinely one small company doing all three, which is more common in West Flanders than a tidy single-sector business. The registry shows the activities and the establishments, and we would rather show you that than present three unrelated brands and hope the connection never comes up.

Got a question before you book?

Ask about allergens, the fire safety certificate, or what you are allowed to tow. No obligation.
